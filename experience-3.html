Tasting with a View
An Exclusive Experience Among the Vineyards

An exclusive experience for those who wish to enjoy Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore D.O.C.G. in its most authentic and evocative expression.

The journey begins with a guided visit to the winery, discovering our production philosophy, the territory, and the Gianfranco Follador Method®. The experience then continues in the heart of the UNESCO World Heritage hills with a transfer to our beautiful Rustico at the foot of the ancient Torri di Credazzo in Farra di Soligo. A private and charming setting surrounded by vineyards, where history, nature, and tradition coexist in perfect harmony.

Here the tasting becomes even more immersive: the wines interact with the landscape, the quiet beauty of the hills accompanies every glass, and time seems to slow down, leaving space for an experience to be enjoyed at a relaxed pace.

Our most exclusive proposal, designed for those who wish to discover both our wines and this remarkable territory from a truly privileged perspective.

General Information

Visiting Hours

Visits are generally scheduled in the morning (10:00/10:30 am) and in the afternoon (3:00/3:30 pm), with some flexibility according to the group’s needs and winery availability. Visits on weekends and public holidays are subject to availability.

Reservations and Cancellations

Advance booking is required and must be made by e-mail. Any changes or cancellations should be communicated at least 48 hours before the experience.

Children and Minors

Children and minors who do not take part in the wine tasting may join free of charge. In the case of food pairings, only the food service fee will be charged.

Food Intolerances and Dietary Requirements

Please inform us of any allergies, dietary restrictions, or special requirements at the time of booking.

Animals

Pets are welcome in the reception and tasting areas; however, access to the production facilities is not permitted for hygiene and safety reasons.
