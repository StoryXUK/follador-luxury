Our Experiences

A winery visit is much more than a tasting: it is an invitation to discover a unique territory through its wines, its people, and its story. Among the UNESCO World Heritage hills of Col San Martino, in the heart of the Conegliano Valdobbiadene D.O.C.G. area, our vision of wine comes to life.

Here we offer curated experiences designed to guide guests in discovering Prosecco Superiore through tastings and breathtaking landscapes that make this land truly one of a kind. We have created a range of experiences to enjoy the winery in an authentic and engaging way, along with bespoke proposals for celebrations, private gatherings, and corporate events.
